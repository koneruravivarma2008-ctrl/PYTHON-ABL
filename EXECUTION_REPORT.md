## Cases covered

- Calculator: addition, division by zero, invalid operator.
- Student grade calculator: Grade A, Grade C, invalid marks.
- Leap year: leap year, century non-leap year, divisible-by-400 leap year.
- Multiplication table: positive, zero, negative number.
- Factorial: positive integer, zero, negative integer.
- Palindrome: palindrome word, phrase with spaces, non-palindrome.
- Vowel/consonant counter: ordinary text, digits/punctuation, all vowels.
- Star pattern: one row, three rows, invalid zero rows.
- List statistics: ordinary values, duplicates, empty list.
- Tuple/set operations: overlapping sets, disjoint sets, repeated values.
